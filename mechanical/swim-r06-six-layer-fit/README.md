# Preliminary fit for r05h

All 69 populated envelopes and 30 analytical shell checks pass against the actual
six-layer r05h board at its nominal 0.8 mm thickness. The smallest checked gap is
C10 to the carrier, 0.24 mm against a 0.20 mm target. The original carrier still
fails the D1 interference control.

This preserves the r02 shell geometry and r05 component-height requirements.
Nine resistor envelopes still require exact part selection. The supplier's actual
six-layer stack and thickness tolerance must be included before mechanical release.

Rigid dimensions are 57.4 × 49.4 × 17.6 mm, or 58.7 mm including the key; the boot is
60.4 × 52.4 × 22.4 mm. The swimming target remains provisionally 2 m fresh water for
60 minutes. No physical dust/water test or new mesh acceptance is claimed.

The inspected populated preview shows conservative component boxes and reserved
pack/antenna volumes. Pack, antennas, harness, dock, positive PCB retention, material
and seal selection remain open. This is a fit review, not a ready-to-order enclosure.

The matching PCB hash and every fit artifact are in `checkpoint.json`. Refresh the
native export, fit checks, shell checks, preview and manifest after any source change.
